NCERT Class 12 Unit 5 Core Concept Summaries, Tables & Formulae

1. Spectrochemical Series (Order of Increasing Field Strength)

I- < Br- < SCN- < Cl- < S2- < F- < OH- < C2O42- < H2O < NCS- < edta4- < NH3 < en < CN- < CO

Condition Ligand Type Octahedral d4 Configuration Spin State
Δo < P Weak field (e.g. F-, Cl-, H2O) t2g3 eg1 High Spin (Spin-free)
Δo > P Strong field (e.g. CN-, CO, en, NH3) t2g4 eg0 Low Spin (Spin-paired)

2. Octahedral vs. Tetrahedral Crystal Field Splitting

Feature Octahedral Field (Δo) Tetrahedral Field (Δt)
Splitting Magnitude Standard reference (Δo) Δt = (4/9) Δo
Splitting Pattern Upper: eg (+0.6 Δo)
Lower: t2g (-0.4 Δo)		 Upper: t2 (+0.4 Δt)
Lower: e (-0.6 Δt)
Complex Spin Type Both High-spin and Low-spin depending on ligand Almost exclusively High-spin (Δt < P)
Centrosymmetric 'g' Tag Used (t2g, eg) due to centre of symmetry Not used (e, t2) because tetrahedral lacks inversion centre

3. Synergic Bonding in Metal Carbonyls

  • M ← C σ-bond: Formed by donation of lone pair on carbon monoxide into a vacant metal d-orbital.
  • M → C π-backbond: Formed by donation of d-electrons from filled metal d-orbital into empty antibonding π* orbital of CO.
  • Synergic Effect: Backbonding into π* orbital strengthens the M-C bond while weakening and elongating the C-O bond.
  • C-O Bond Order Ranking: Greater negative charge on complex → greater back-donation → weakest/longest C-O bond:
    [V(CO)6]- < [Cr(CO)6] < [Mn(CO)6]+.
